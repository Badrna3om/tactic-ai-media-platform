# The 90-second TACTIC tour

**For hiring managers, recruiters and business leaders.** You do not need to know n8n, SQL or AI APIs to understand this project.

## 0–15 seconds: what is it?

**TACTIC × AMER** is a real sports-media production operation built around connected AI-assisted workflows. It produces **multiple branded editorial, visual and video formats** from structured event sources.

[Watch the animated 15-second preview](../assets/production-reel.webp).

## 15–45 seconds: one event, several products

A real Barcelona–Villarreal match produced separate, published outputs:

1. [Starting XI](https://x.com/TacticSpoort/status/2124483139678384632) — team rosters and match presentation
2. [Match statistics](https://x.com/TacticSpoort/status/2124525366769234248) — comparative performance data
3. [Player-of-the-match card](https://x.com/TacticSpoort/status/2124530161156510070) — a dedicated player product

These are **three independent content products**, not three copies of the same post. They illustrate one match journey; not every match is guaranteed to produce every format.

## 65–90 seconds: why an employer should care

This platform demonstrates:

- Multi-step process design instead of disconnected automations
- Database-backed state, approvals and reliable handoffs
- AI working **inside defined business rules**
- Reusable connectors, media services and recovery paths
- Coordination across multiple output types and brand identities

**Business translation:** replace sports events with leads, orders, documents, service requests or internal alerts. The same input-to-decision-to-action architecture applies to many industries.
