## 4. AMER branded production

Separate production paths generate matchday **fixtures**, **results**, selected round/coach-related visuals, news-layout corrections, and video-preparation deliverables under AMER's branding.

**Public reference:** [AMER's Snapchat profile (@amer.aaa)](https://www.snapchat.com/@amer.aaa).

The AMER motion flows can receive a video link or uploaded clip, prepare a formatted vertical video, and deliver the MP4 via Telegram. **Automatic posting to Snapchat was not independently verified** and is not claimed here. No private Telegram chats, unpublished files, sponsor assets or client dashboards are shared.

## 5. Video / motion infrastructure

Production components cover:
- Identification and cataloging of source videos.
- Linking goal-media candidates to relevant match events.
- Flagging and resolving missing match-media assets.
- Rendering vertical goal-related video deliverables.
- Delivery and operational recovery handling.

**Indexed source videos are not equivalent to completed reels or published Snapchat videos.** See [Production Evidence](PRODUCTION_EVIDENCE.md).
